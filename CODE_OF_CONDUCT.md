# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge
We want taking part in CheckAm to be free of harassment for everyone, whatever their age, body, ability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience, education, social or economic status, nationality, language, appearance, race, religion or sexual identity and orientation.

## What we expect
- Be kind and patient, especially with people reporting that they were scammed. Many lost money they could not afford.
- Give and accept feedback with respect, and focus on what is best for the people CheckAm protects.
- Never share anyone's phone number, messages or personal details without their consent.

## What is not accepted
- Insults, harassment, trolling or sexual language and imagery.
- Blaming or mocking scam victims.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement
Report problems to groundtruth.cm@gmail.com. Every report is reviewed and kept confidential. The maintainers may remove comments, code or issues that break this code, and may ban anyone who keeps breaking it, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
